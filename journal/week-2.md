# Reflection Journal

## Week of: September 23, 2026 to September 27, 2026

## My goal this week
My main objective was to transition from the initial setup and mockups of Week 1 to actually building out the core UI for all remaining screens. I also aimed to establish the foundational backend integrations for the Canvas LMS and the generative AI system.

## What I did
I successfully built out the UI for basically all of the application's screens. On the backend side, I implemented the Canvas REST API integration and hooked up the Gemini AI model. I also focused on applying general design tweaks across the app to ensure consistency and implemented a strict local-first caching strategy to keep the data layer lightweight. 

## What blocked me
I actually didn't face any major blockers this week. Because I am currently focused on laying out the foundational bases and backend systems for everything, progress has been smooth and steady without any major roadblocks.

## What I learned
I learned the value of solidifying the backend structure and data handling early on. By deciding to stick strictly to a local-first caching strategy instead of a cloud database, I realized how much overhead can be avoided, keeping the app fast and lightweight. This solid foundation will make it much easier to tackle the complex state management needed when I integrate the tailored AI responses and the automated Planner hub next week.
